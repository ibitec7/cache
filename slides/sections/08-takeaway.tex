\section{Takeaway}

\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Model the reuse-distance hazard, not the aggregate hit count.
    \item The LRU stack property turns survival into the whole curve at once.
    \item Capacity becomes an evaluation point, so unmeasured capacities are
      reachable.
    \item Censoring is the main statistical hazard, in both senses.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1.5em}
  \begin{block}{One sentence}
    A censored discrete hazard over reuse distance estimates the miss-ratio
    curve at every capacity from a single fit.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
