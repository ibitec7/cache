\section{Limitations}

\begin{frame}{Limitations}
  \begin{description}
    \item[Trace vintage] 2020 traces. A description of that period, not a
      forecast.
    \item[Single vendor] One production fleet. Cross-vendor transfer untested.
    \item[Hazard binning] $K$ bins bound curve resolution, and bin edges matter
      near the knee.
    \item[Replacement policy] Identification is LRU. Segmented or sampled LRU
      does not satisfy a pure distance threshold.
  \end{description}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What would change the conclusion}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A policy-aware recency structure in place of the threshold condition.
    \item A continuous hazard to remove the binning limit.
    \item A per-cluster transfer check within the fleet.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{block}{Falsifier}
    If estimated curves miss the simulated curve by more than the simulation
    band at the knee, the survival formulation does not pay for itself.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
