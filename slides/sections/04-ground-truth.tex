\section{Ground truth}

\begin{frame}{Simulation is the reference}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Full simulation of the held-out segment on a fixed capacity grid.
    \item Exact stack distance where the trace fits in memory.
    \item SHARDS with a fixed sampling rate where it does not.
    \item The sampling rate and its confidence band ship with every curve.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{block}{Rule}
    Model error is never reported below simulation error.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Metrics}
  \begin{description}
    \item[MRC error] max and mean absolute deviation from the simulated curve
      over the capacity grid.
    \item[Calibration] reliability of hazard predictions, summarized as
      expected calibration error.
    \item[Brier / IBS] squared error of the hazard, integrated over bins.
  \end{description}
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{block}{Why not likelihood}
    Likelihood rewards over-confident hazards; the Brier score does not.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
